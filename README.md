# academic_writing_polish_eng
A Codex skill for researchers writing engineering papers in English. It draws on recurring writing patterns in Prof. Xiaowei Wang’s publications in bridge and earthquake engineering, including how the papers introduce modeling assumptions, compare structural responses across loading conditions, and distinguish observed results from possible mechanisms. English Only!

The skill can draft English prose from Chinese research notes, polish an existing paragraph with minimal changes, edit a full section, compare alternative phrasings, or review a manuscript for consistency. Its editing rules preserve numerical values, units, notation, technical terms, and the level of certainty supported by the original evidence.
